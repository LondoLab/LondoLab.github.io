## Overview

Grapevine and apple are highly heterozygous and propagated as clones, so a single cultivar can be grown for centuries across very different sites. We build haplotype resolved genomes to read that heterozygosity, and we use small RNA and methylation sequencing to ask how the epigenome of a clone responds to the place it grows.
