Kenneth earned his PhD studying rootstock × scion effects on apple bud cold hardiness through electrolyte leakage assays, multispectral aerial drone imaging, and RNA-seq across multiple growing seasons in New York apple orchards. He is now a postdoctoral scholar with Dr. Lee Kalcsits at Washington State University.
