Faith was a Masters student that was co-advised with Dr. Katie Gold. Her Masters work focused on understanding the spectral response of grapevine cultivars to heat and drought stresses. She compared the impact of these stresses on plant physiological traits, leaf nutrient composition, isotope ratios, and metabolites.
