Faith was a Masters student that was co-advised with Dr. Katie Gold (Plant Pathology). She earned her Masters examining the spectral responses of grapevine cultivars to heat and drought stresses. She compared the impact of these stresses on plant physiological traits, leaf nutrient composition, isotope ratios, and metabolites.
